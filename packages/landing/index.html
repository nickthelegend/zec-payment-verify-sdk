Frequently asked questions

What a viewing key shows, and what the check trusts.

What does my viewing key reveal?

Every incoming payment to its wallet, and nothing that can spend. Share it only with whoever should see those payments. The keys published here are demo-only.

What does the check trust?

lightwalletd, not to withhold or lag transactions. The checker recomputes every txid, so it cannot be handed a different transaction. The paywall keeps memo nonces and used txids in memory.

Does a null-402 proof fully pass?

Not live yet: BLOCKED. A proof that passes all three checks needs null-402's pool operator to register a new root, and that key (NULL402_OPERATOR_KEY) is not held.

Can I install it from npm?

Not yet: publishing to npm and crates.io needs the maintainer's accounts. It installs from the packed tarball. Testnet only, not audited.
